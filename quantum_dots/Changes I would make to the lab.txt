- [ ] Use scipy.optimize.curve_fit rather than a helper function for realism
- [ ] Would like to organize the provided PIB and Brus' equations so that the curve fit parameters are Ebulk and the effective mass of the electron / hole.

Epib(r) = Egap + (pi**2)/(8*m_e*r)**2)
(where Egap (bulk bandgap) and m_e (effective mass of electron) are the optimizable parameters)
(in atomic units, where l = 2*r)

and then Brus' model where we add a hole with its own effective mass
Ebruh(r) = Egap + (pi**2)/(8 * r**2) * (1/m_e + 1/m_h)
where Egap, m_e and m_h (effective mass of hole) are the optimizable parameters
Brus' model has r**2 dependence of both electron and hole!

  - reason being, students can think about model curve fitting optimization not just as a tool for making predictions, but also the optimized parameters themselves as having physical interpretation (bandgap, effective mass, etc)
