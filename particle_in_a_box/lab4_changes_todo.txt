overall thoughts / feedback
- content choice is good. teaching students about numerical optimization routines and letting them work with an example is good.
- also good to flag data types. probably something we should have done near the start of the semester...
- good description of Hartree-Fock. just worry the students won't have the bandwidth for it. We can approach from a higher level of abstraction for this purpose
- descriptions should be short and have friendly formatting (bulleted lists etc). students just won't read them if they're too long in my experience.
- We should let students discover the result for themselves rather than telling them what it will be, as much as possible. If we want them to notice something, just make it the topic of an SRQ. Well-scaffolded exploration is flagged as leading to better learning outcomes in literature (and it is less writing for us!)
